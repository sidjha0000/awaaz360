🩹 Basic First Aid Tips

  • For Burns: Cool the burn with water for 10-15 minutes.
  • For Cuts: Clean with water, apply pressure, and cover with a clean cloth.
  • For Fainting: Lay the person down and elevate legs.
  • For Snake Bites: Keep the area still and seek medical help immediately.
