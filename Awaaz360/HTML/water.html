Water Conservation

💧 Water is life. Conserving water ensures a future for our planet, people, and agriculture.

🌊 Why Water Conservation Matters

Only 2.5% of Earth’s water is freshwater. With growing population and pollution, protecting this resource is crucial for drinking, farming, and ecosystems.

🚿 Water-Saving Tips for Households

  • Turn off the tap while brushing teeth or washing dishes.
  • Fix leaking taps and toilets immediately.
  • Use low-flow showerheads and water-efficient appliances.
  • Collect rainwater for gardening or cleaning purposes.

🚜 Agricultural Water Management

  • Adopt drip irrigation or sprinkler systems to reduce wastage.
  • Use mulching to retain soil moisture.
  • Choose drought-resistant crop varieties.
  • Recycle wastewater safely for non-edible crops.

🏞️ Rainwater Harvesting

A traditional yet powerful method. Collect rainwater through rooftop catchment or surface runoff into tanks or underground reservoirs.

  • Improves groundwater levels.
  • Reduces flooding and soil erosion.
  • Can be used during dry months.

🏛️ Government Initiatives

  • Jal Shakti Abhiyan – water conservation campaign.
  • Atal Bhujal Yojana – improve groundwater management.
  • MGNREGA – water conservation activities in rural jobs program.

👥 Community Participation

Educate others about water saving. Participate in lake clean-ups, waterbody revival, and support local conservation drives. Every drop counts!
