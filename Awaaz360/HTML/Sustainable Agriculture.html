Sustainable Agriculture

🌾 Sustainable agriculture is the key to a greener, healthier future — preserving resources while feeding the world.

🌱 What is Sustainable Agriculture?

Sustainable agriculture focuses on producing food while maintaining environmental balance, improving soil health, and ensuring fair treatment of farmers.

📌 Key Principles

  • ✅ Soil Conservation
  • ✅ Water Efficiency
  • ✅ Crop Diversity
  • ✅ Low Chemical Usage
  • ✅ Livelihood Support for Farmers

🛠️ Eco-Friendly Farming Practices

  • 🌾 Crop Rotation & Mixed Farming
  • 🍀 Use of Organic Fertilizers & Compost
  • 🐞 Natural Pest Control (Neem oil, companion planting)
  • 🚜 Precision Agriculture (sensor-based irrigation, GPS mapping)
  • 💧 Drip & Sprinkler Irrigation Systems

🌾 Benefits of Sustainable Agriculture

  • ✅ Improves soil fertility & biodiversity
  • ✅ Reduces pollution & greenhouse gas emissions
  • ✅ Saves water and energy
  • ✅ Enhances farmer income & food security

🏛️ Government Schemes for Farmers

  • PM-KISAN: Direct income support to farmers.
  • Soil Health Card: Personalized soil nutrient advice.
  • PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana: Efficient irrigation support.
  • Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY): Innovation funding.

📈 Role of Technology

From drones and soil sensors to AI-driven crop analysis, tech enables precision, boosts productivity, and reduces waste in agriculture.
